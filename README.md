# Features

- New material processing chains for cleaning and refining resources, making reinforced components, and producing specialty fluids.
- Custom processing machines, with tiers available for selected machines. Machine stats and tiers vary by prototype.
- Additional power generation, energy storage, and power distribution equipment.
- Configurable build, interaction, and resource reach bonuses through Mod settings.
- An in-game calculator with calculation history, accessible from its shortcut.
- Quality mod support for quality modules in supported machines.
- Expanded item and fluid storage, plus custom logistic and construction robots.
- A selection tool for removing trees and supported rock entities.
- Trains: [Red Railworld Extended](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RERailworld)

## Energy

#### Power Generation

- **Steam boilers R2 and R3**: The prototypes specify 15 MW and 60 MW of heating capacity, respectively.
- **Steam engines R2 and R3**: Higher-tier steam engines with custom fluid handling and effectivity settings. Their prototypes do not specify fixed 3.6 MW or 7.2 MW output ratings.
- **Solar panels R2–R5**: 600 kW, 6 MW, 36 MW, and 150 MW production, respectively.
- **Solar panel equipment R2**: 100 kW for armor equipment grids.
- **Accumulators R2 and R3**: 30 MJ and 50 MJ capacity, respectively.

#### Power Distribution

The mod adds small, medium, and long-range pole variants, a substation variant, and a light pole. The custom substation is R1; there are no R2 or R3 substation prototypes.

## Machines and Objects

#### Processing Machines

The mod adds or extends the following processing equipment:

- Compressor
- Washer chamber
- Water condenser
- Mixer
- Cast chamber
- Heat forge chamber
- Chemical machine
- Enrichment chamber
- Refinery
- Centrifuge R2
- Air purification equipment
- Furnaces, assemblers, mining equipment, and a beacon

Some machines have additional tiers, but tiers and specifications differ by machine. Check the in-game recipe and machine tooltip for its crafting speed, power use, fluid connections, and module slots.

#### Storage and logistics

- **Warehouses** for item storage.
- **Big storage tanks** for fluid storage, with multiple tiers.
- **Advanced Pump**: moves up to 4,800 fluid/s, with an 800-unit internal capacity and 175 kW active power use.
- Custom logistic and construction robots, logistic chests, and a roboport.
- A custom radar.

#### Pipes

- **Copper Pipe** and **Iron Pipe**, with matching underground variants.
- Each pipe has a fluid capacity of 250; the underground pipes connect across a distance of up to 21 tiles.

## Materials and fluids

The added recipes include:

- **Clean materials**: iron, copper, coal, and steel.
- **Components**: glue, cable, reinforced components and plates, reinforced gears, and copper gear wheels.
- **Coal products**: Clean Coal (16 MJ), Infused Coal (72 MJ), Enriched Coal (144 MJ), and Infused Enriched Coal (288 MJ). Clean Coal is made from 2 Coal and 20 Pressurized Water in a Washer Chamber, providing twice the coal inputs' combined fuel energy before processing costs.
- **Infusion tools**: the Infusion Tool is used to make Infused Coal. Through Advanced Infusion research, 2 Enriched Coal and 1 Advanced Infusion Tool produce 2 Infused Enriched Coal in 4 seconds in a Formation Furnace: 576 MJ of output fuel from 288 MJ in coal inputs, before processing costs. The tool is consumed.
- **Rocket Fuel alternatives**: three Enrichment Chamber recipes use Clean Coal R1 with heavy oil, Infused Coal with diesel, or Enriched Coal. They produce 1, 2, or 3 Rocket Fuel, respectively.
- **Specialty fluids**: pressurized water, molten iron, copper, steel, and coal, diesel fuel, enriched petroleum, lithium, and other processing fluids.
- **Crystalline silicon products** for the solar panel production chain.

These materials are used in the mod's machinery, power, storage, and robot recipes.

## Quality of Life

 - **Exoskeleton R2**:
 A late-game 2×4 equipment module that provides a 100% movement speed bonus.
 It frees armor-grid space compared with installing several vanilla exoskeletons, at the cost of drawing up to 1 MW while active.

#### Research

Optional research chains improve movement speed, inventory capacity, crafting speed, and mining speed.
Each chain can be enabled or disabled in the startup settings.

#### Starting Robots

When enabled in the startup settings, players begin with modular armor, construction robots, roboports, batteries, and portable solar panels.

#### Tree and Rock Eraser

The tree-rock-eraser selection tool removes trees and supported rock entities in the selected area.
Rock support includes entities from the base game and Alien Biomes when installed.
It does not remove arbitrary entities.

#### Calculator

An in-game calculator with calculation history, accessible from its shortcut.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Defense

- **Laser turret R2**: A more powerful laser turret with high energy consumption.

## Features Removed after Factorio 2.0

- **Transport Belts**: Improved belt systems for material transport, with higher throughput and specialized variants.
- **Incinerator**: Waste disposal and resource recovery, converting unwanted items into useful resources.
- **Bio-Red**: Biological processing systems, using living organisms for specialized production processes.

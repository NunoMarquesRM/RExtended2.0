Version: 2.5.8
Date: 22.11.2026
  Info:
    - This version changes machine recipes and technology prerequisites.
      Existing production chains may need adjustments after updating.
      Back up your save before updating and review the changes below.
  Major Features:
    - Added Azurium, Apatitium, and Amazonium crystals and plates with three research tiers.
    - Added Advanced and Elite Electronic Components, three Fuse tiers, and three Machine Casings.
      Machine Casings have a dedicated item subgroup.
    - Added Advanced Infusion research, the Advanced Infusion Tool, and Infused Enriched Coal.
      The new fuel provides 288 MJ per item and stacks to 50.
    - Applied Advanced and Elite naming to the new item families across all four locales.
  Changes:
    - Technology:
      - Elite Electric Mining Drill now requires Amazonium research and Military Science Pack.
      - Advanced Electric Mining Drill now requires the vanilla drill and Apatitium research.
      - Elite Storage Tank now requires Amazonium research and Space Science Pack.
      - Advanced Storage Tank now requires Apatitium research and its missing science unlocks.
      - Large Storage Tank now requires Mixer Products and Chemical Science Pack.
      - Warehouse research now requires Advanced Intermediate Products and Chemical Science Pack.
      - Powerful Transmission now requires Advanced Intermediate Products 2;
      research cost increases from 200 to 500 units.
      - Advanced Craft Machine now requires Advanced Intermediate Products 3 and Utility Science
      Pack alongside Automation 3 and Craft Machine R1 research.
      - Reworked machine research paths to unlock required materials and processing machines first.
      - Connected Azurium-based machines to Advanced Intermediate Products and Apatitium-based
      upgrades to Advanced Intermediate Products 2.
      - Added missing science-pack prerequisites and removed redundant research links.
      - Oil Special Processing now requires Steam Power for its steam-based recipes.
      - Advanced Centrifuge now requires the Amazonium material tier and missing science unlocks
      instead of Advanced Compact Craft Machine research.
      - Silicon Processing now requires Refined Coal; Lithium now requires Advanced Ore Processing
      R1 and Refinery R1 for its production chain.
      - Chemical Machine R1 research time increases from 25 to 30 seconds per unit.
    - Recipes:
      - Elite Electric Mining Drill now uses Elite Fuses while retaining its predecessor drill,
      reinforced plates, and cables.
      - Advanced Electric Mining Drill now uses an Advanced Machine Casing and Advanced Fuses.
      - Elite Storage Tank now uses Elite casings and fuses,
      retaining three Advanced Storage Tanks as inputs.
      - Advanced Storage Tank now uses Apatitium casings and fuses,
      retaining five Large Storage Tanks as inputs.
      - Warehouse construction now uses Azurium casings and components alongside reinforced plates.
      - Beacon R1 now uses Apatitium-based casings, electronic components, and fuses.
      - Advanced Centrifuge now uses Elite casings and fuses while retaining two base centrifuges.
      - Advanced Craft Machine now uses Amazonium-based Elite parts and its previous-tier machine,
      retaining electric engines and a 5-second crafting time.
      - Reworked Craft Machine R1, Chemical Machine R1, Refinery R1, Enrichment Chamber,
      Heat Forge R1, and Cast Chamber R1 to use Azurium-based casings and fuses.
      - Reworked the Advanced Compact Craft Machine, Advanced Chemical Machine, Advanced Water
      Condenser, Compressor R2, Washer Chamber R2, Heat Forge R2, Cast Chamber R2, and Refinery R2
      to use Apatitium-based intermediates and their previous-tier machines.
      - Boiler R3 and Steam Engine R3 now use Apatitium-based intermediates;
      crafting takes 6 and 8 seconds, respectively.
      - Balanced revised machine costs against their previous expanded material requirements.
      Craft Machine R1 retains three Compact Craft Machines as inputs.
      - Charcoal now requires furnace smelting: 4 Wood yield 1 Coal in 3 seconds.
      - Reduced Clean Coal's fuel value from 36 MJ to 16 MJ.
      - Advanced infusion consumes its tool to double Enriched Coal's total fuel energy:
      2 Enriched Coal yield 2 Infused Enriched Coal, providing 576 MJ before processing costs.
  Graphics:
    - Refined Infused Enriched Coal with infusion veins and a mottled enriched surface.
    - Refined Blue Quartz's icon with clearer icy-blue crystals and dark mineral inclusions.
    - Refined Crystalline Silicon's icon with metallic shards and a subtle blue sheen.
    - Updated Silicon Processing technology artwork with Blue Quartz and crystalline silicon.
    - Updated Infusion Tool icon to a shared industrial design.
    - Refined Infused Coal's icon with softer golden veins, clearer carbon texture.
    - Updated Infused Products technology artwork to match its coal and basic infusion tool.
    - Refined Enriched Coal's icon with teal-amber petroleum mottling and no lettering.
    - Refined Clean Coal's inventory and technology artwork with charcoal grain and no lettering.
    - Redesigned Enrichment Chamber R1 with a compact central vessel and gold-and-silver housing.
